A program that draws one 1024×1024 image summing up the world news of
Tuesday, 24 November 2026, as a newspaper front page: a masthead with the
date, one lead story, four stories in a grid, and a closing strip.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.png`. No network, no clock, no randomness: running
it twice writes byte-identical output.

## The six stories

1. **Nvidia** — Jensen Huang is reinstated as CEO after Trump steps in;
   shares jump 33%.
2. **Kyiv region** — Russian missile and drone strikes kill at least 14.
3. **Grok** — US states sue Musk's firms over sexualised images made by
   the chatbot.
4. **South Korea** — evacuation warning on Ulleung island after a Russian
   aircraft enters its airspace.
5. **Philippines** — Trump offers Manila $2.4bn for self-defence.
6. **United Nations** — the Secretary-General calls for AI risk rules
   within a year.

Sources: Reuters, Associated Press, Agence France-Presse, South China
Morning Post.
